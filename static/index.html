LITTLE ABOUT

Who We Are

Transporter Intelligence is an electronic platform established under the Nigerian Gas Transportation Network Code (NGTNC) for the efficient oversight of NGIC transportation submissions, NEA compliance, shipper accountability, and offtaker attribution reporting across Nigeria's domestic gas network.

The platform centralises data from all licensed pipeline operators and enables regulators to monitor injection volumes, exceptions, and compliance scores in real time.

WHAT WE DO

NMDPRA Services

NMDPRA has the statutory responsibility of ensuring compliance to petroleum laws, regulations and guidelines in the Oil and Gas Industry.

SUBMISSION

Data Submission

Issuance and management of NGIC transportation submissions covering injection volumes, offtaker attributions, and imbalance records under the NC regime.

COMPLIANCE

Compliance Monitoring

Real-time compliance scoring, exception detection, and threshold breach alerts across all pipeline operators and supplier GASCOs.

REPORTING

Regulatory Reporting

Multi-view reporting by shipper, gas supplier/GASCO, offtaker, sector, and exception workflow with full audit trail, daily series charts, and export capabilities.
